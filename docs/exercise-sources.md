<a id="qué-significa-la-rutina"></a>

## Routine meaning

Two sets/6–10 reps (calves10–15)/initial60-second rest/demo tempo editorial base,
not individual prescription/validated clinical protocol. Pike4–8/90 seconds,
higher demand. Reduce range/reps/difficulty for control/rest more as needed.
Dead bug/bird dog/reverse-lateral lunge/climber per-side6 means12 alternating;
rep includes side gesture/return, full visual cycle both. Within-rep pauses do not
change unit to seconds; text tempo guidance/viewer approximate/pause-speed controls.
Heel taps/quadruped legs/marching bridge alternate; static lunge/single calf/bridge
finish side first, viewer explicitly one side. Marching bridge foot up-return=one
side rep, hips held changes no unit. Harder variants own4–8/5–8/6–10 dose, never inherited.

Two nonconsecutive weekly sessions/gradual progression. [CDC adult guidance:≥two
strength days/150 moderate aerobic minutes or higher-intensity equivalent](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html)
population context, no immediate individual requirement. App minutes no assumed intensity/
automatic compliance. No-equipment push/squat/hip/trunk/scapula/ankle **not fully
balanced strength**: light W not resisted row/pull-up. Progressive lat-biceps
pulling pending equipment; no improvised home anchor suggestions.

## Anatomical model scope

Groups/colors educational simplification. Main prominent movers/control regions,
secondary assistants/stabilizers; no EMG/activation percentage/diagnosis/load/
individual anatomy. Upper back trapezius-rhomboid; no separate rotator cuff/
spinal erector-deep/adductor/hip-flexor layers. Bird dog/good morning/lateral lunge/
climber notes explain omissions. Colored regions do not prove stimulus sufficiency/
volume balance. Procedural animation, not motion capture; personal range may
vary, no camera posture analysis/personal technique correction.
