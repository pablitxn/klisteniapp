# Yoga library: editorial criteria and sources

Review October 7, 2026. `apps/b2c-web/src/data/yoga.ts`:24 asanas/three editable
practices, searchable Spanish/transliterated Sanskrit/English.

<a id="alcance-y-criterio"></a>

## Scope and criteria

Rioplatense Spanish product content; brief adapted synthesis, no source text/photos/
illustrations reproduced. Sensations possible, not guaranteed. Cards distinguish
setup/movement/exit/breath/sensations/errors/adaptations/cautions/name-teaching
history/cultural context/own references. No invented posture age. Hanumān/Vīrabhadra/
Matsyendra cultural narratives, not verified biography/invention date.

Durations/three sequences app editorial, not clinical/traditional school protocol;
reduce/order/skip. Advertised time holds only, setup/transitions additional.
Unilateral side semantics/equal template times. Level variant complexity, not
personal worth/spiritual advance; beginner may need adaptation. Avoid head-neck
inversions/lotus/forced breathing as entry.

<a id="marco-histórico-y-cultural"></a>

## Historical and cultural framework

- [UNESCO: El yoga](https://ich.unesco.org/es/RL/el-yoga-01163): India/diverse beyond-posture practice/cultural transmission, not medical-effect evaluation.
- [SOAS Hatha Yoga Project](https://www.soas.ac.uk/research/hatha-yoga-project-ancient-practices-modern-wellbeing): text-fieldwork history/time-changing practice, not every catalog form's date.
- [Zo Newell: The Mythology Behind Anjaneyasana](https://yogainternational.com/article/view/the-mythology-behind-anjaneyasana-low-lunge/): Añjanā/Hanumān narrative explicitly.
- [Sandra Anderson: Legend of the Spinal Twist](https://yogainternational.com/article/view/legend-of-the-spinal-twist/): contemporary Matsyendra telling, therapeutic claims not evidence.

<a id="seguridad-y-lenguaje-sobre-salud"></a>

## Safety and health language

- [NCCIH: Yoga, Effectiveness and Safety](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/yoga-effectiveness-and-safety): qualified teacher/injury risk/individual health adaptations.
- [NCCIH: 5 Things You Should Know About Yoga](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/tips/things-you-should-know-about-yoga): pregnancy adaptation/prolonged supine time.

Instruction sources may mix technique/testimony/energy/disease claims. Consulted
for posture/movement/support, not healing/detox/emotional release/organ regulation.
Cautions general, not exhaustive contraindications/personal assessment.
